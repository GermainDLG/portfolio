An AI weather startup is now out-forecasting government agencies
AI Breakfast <aibreakfast@mail.beehiiv.com>

WindBorne Systems, an AI weather forecasting startup that has raised $25M in venture funding, released WeatherMesh-6, a new model that outperforms the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts by producing hourly predictions with 3km resolution. The company differentiates itself through a proprietary data advantage from its fleet of 400 weather balloons feeding directly into its transformer-based deep learning model.
